# Milestone 11 expansion decision record

Status: deferred until launch and observed demand. No candidate is selected.

The milestone plan lists personalized recommendations, loyalty points, paid
membership, seller advertising, wallet or credit enhancements, grocery and
rapid delivery, advanced logistics, and native apps as candidates. Their order
cannot be chosen responsibly before launch traffic, conversion, repeat
purchase, delivery quality, support load, and seller adoption can be measured.

For each candidate, the product owner should record:

1. The customer or seller problem and evidence from launch data or interviews.
2. A measurable outcome, baseline, target, and review date.
3. Expected operational cost, dependencies, and legal or provider obligations.
4. A narrow first release, its acceptance checks, and a rollback path.
5. The decision to build, defer, or reject, with the responsible owner.

The first expansion feature should be selected only after Milestone 10 launch
acceptance and a review of real marketplace demand. No expansion code is
started on this branch.
