# Trust and merchandising milestone status

Milestone 9 has a wishlist record and a review submission service. Reviews
require a paid, delivered order line for the product. New reviews enter a
moderation queue; staff can change moderation status in Django admin. Automated
tests cover purchase gating, duplicate submissions, and wishlist idempotency.

Remaining work: buyer-facing wishlist and review screens, ratings display,
product questions and moderation, product comparison, coupons and campaigns,
homepage merchandising, recommendations, and promotion calculations in
checkout. The milestone is not accepted.
